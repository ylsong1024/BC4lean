## Cycle fidelity

For trivially graded A and B, an even cycle uses a complete countably generated
right Hilbert B-module E with grading J, compatible semilinear group action U
commuting with J, an even equivariant possibly degenerate nonunital star
representation φ, and an odd adjointable operator F. The four conditions are,
for all a and g:

- `(F - F*) φ(a)` is compact;
- `(F² - 1) φ(a)` is compact;
- `F φ(a) - φ(a) F` is compact;
- `(U_g F U_g⁻¹ - F) φ(a)` is compact.

No strict Γ-invariance of F or nondegeneracy of φ is imposed. The odd raw-cycle
picture omits J; its equivalence to the Clifford-graded picture is not asserted.
For nontrivial coefficient gradings/actions, J and U_g are correctly
semilinear over B; they are not treated as B-linear adjointable operators.

The compact module ideal is not defined using finite-dimensional complex
ranges. On B_B, rank-one maps are L_(x y*) and the full compact ideal is B,
proved using an approximate unit and the closed isometric left-multiplication
range. Thus the standard identity-cycle construction does not assume its own
compactness condition, and does not require a unit in B.

### Creation-map correction

The exact issue is in arXiv:1703.10912v2, printed **page 13** (PDF index 12),
Section 3.3, the unnumbered **S-connection** paragraph immediately before
Definition 3.10: “Then θξ ∈ K(F, F ⊗B E) with adjoint given by”. The PDF was
rendered and visually inspected: the printed symbol really is K. The tensor
factors are also reversed there, compared with the preceding map
θξ : F → E ⊗B F.

The context uses an A–B Kasparov cycle (E,u,Φ,T) and a B–C cycle (F,v,Ψ,S).
It imposes no compactness of Ψ(B); Remark 3.11(b) adds that hypothesis later
for a special case. Thus unconditional compactness is an actual false printed
claim, rather than a PDF extraction error or a missing assumption in our
formalization.

A counterexample satisfying the preceding cycle hypotheses has trivial G and
A=B=C=ℂ. Take E=ℂ with all-even grading, scalar representation and T=0. Its
square defect is finite rank. Take F=ℓ²(ℕ)⊕ℓ²(ℕ) with its Hilbert direct-sum
norm, grading J(x,y)=(x,−y), scalar representation and S(x,y)=(y,x). It is
complete and countably generated by the two delta families; S*=S, S²=1 and
JS=−SJ, so every defect vanishes. Creation at 1 identifies with id_F under
ℂ ⊗ F ≅ F and is not compact because F is infinite dimensional.

The corrected general starting statement is

```text
θξ ∈ L_C(F, E ⊗_Ψ F),
θξ*(ζ ⊗ η) = Ψ(⟨ξ,ζ⟩_B)η,
θξ* θζ = Ψ(⟨ξ,ζ⟩_B).
```

The inner-product identity verifies the adjoint formula on pure tensors;
Cauchy–Schwarz gives the bound and density extends it to the completed interior
tensor product. Compactness requires its own proof and valid assumptions.
In particular Ψ(B)⊆K_C(F) is sufficient, using the last identity and the
rectangular criterion that T is compact iff T*T is compact. That criterion is
now strictly proved in `CompactModuleOperatorCriterion`, with complete source.
The general interior tensor completion and creation map remain separate formal
work; neither their existence nor their norm is assumed in this batch.
