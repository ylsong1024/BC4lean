\chapter{Scope, conventions, and prerequisites}
\label{ch:scope}

\section{The version of the conjecture}
The main target is the Baum--Connes conjecture for all countable discrete
groups, without coefficients, using the reduced group $C^*$-algebra.
The degree is $i\in\{0,1\}$. All algebras and Hilbert spaces are complex.
Write $\Gamma$ for a variable group; no particular matrix group is built
into the definitions.

\section{Standing assumptions}
The group algebra, the regular representation, and the reduced group
$C^*$-algebra make sense for arbitrary discrete groups. Impose countability
for the main conjecture and the chosen separable KK-theoretic route.
Specify all local compactness, Hausdorffness, and second-countability
assumptions on spaces where they are needed.

\section{Background interfaces}
Organize prerequisites for Hilbert spaces, bounded operators, complex
$C^*$-algebras (including nonunital algebras), functions vanishing at infinity,
gradings, and filtered colimits of abelian groups.

\section{Status conventions}
This is a structural draft, not a completed mathematical blueprint.
Nodes marked as not ready are specifications to expand. Lean verification
currently covers the concrete reduced group $C^*$-algebra construction,
starting from the group algebra and the $\ell^2$ regular representation,
together with its generating property, countable-group separability, and
the trivial-group identification, and the canonical isometric star-algebra
isomorphism from the abstract reduced-norm completion. Operator K-theory verification now covers projections, Murray--von Neumann
equivalence, matrix block sums and stabilization, and fixed-size norm homotopy
of unitaries. The stable quotients, $K_0$, $K_1$, their structural properties,
and the assembly construction remain unverified. Separate construction of the objects,
formalization of the conjecture's statement, and proof of the conjecture.
