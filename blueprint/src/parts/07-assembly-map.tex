\chapter{Construction of the assembly map}
\label{ch:assembly-map}

Let $\Gamma$ be a countable discrete group for the KK-theoretic targets.
The crossed-product and cutoff prerequisites below are proved for arbitrary
discrete groups unless countability is stated explicitly. Coefficient
C$^*$-algebras may be nonunital. The four unfinished descent and assembly
targets retain their full mathematical statements.

\section{Reduced crossed products and descent}
\begin{definition}\label{def:algebraic-crossed-product}
\uses{def:equivariant-kk}
\lean{BC4lean.KKTheory.AlgebraicCrossedProduct,BC4lean.KKTheory.AlgebraicCrossedProduct.coeff_mul,BC4lean.KKTheory.AlgebraicCrossedProduct.coeff_star}
\leanok
For an action $\alpha$ of $\Gamma$ by complex star automorphisms of $B$,
form the actual finite-support coefficient star algebra $C_c(\Gamma,B)$.
Its operations are
\[
 (f*h)(k)=\sum_g f(g)\alpha_g(h(g^{-1}k)),\qquad
 f^*(k)=\alpha_k(f(k^{-1})^*).
\]
The sum is finite, and associativity and the star-algebra laws are proved
for these operations.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:regular-crossed-hilbert-module}
\lean{BC4lean.KKTheory.ReducedCrossedHilbertModule,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedFiniteInner_self_eq_zero_iff,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedMk_denseRange,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedMk_norm_coefficients}
\leanok
Complete the finite vectors $\Gamma\to B$ in the genuine right Hilbert
$B$-module norm
\[
 \|\xi\|=\left\|\sum_g\xi(g)^*\xi(g)\right\|^{1/2}.
\]
The finite-vector coefficient-valued form is positive and definite; its
null radical is proved zero. The canonical finite-vector image in the
completed module $\ell^2(\Gamma,B)$ is dense.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:regular-crossed-operators}
\uses{def:regular-crossed-hilbert-module}
\lean{BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedCoefficient,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedRegular,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedEvaluation_coefficient,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedEvaluation_regular,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossed_covariance,BC4lean.KKTheory.norm_reducedCrossedCoefficient}
\leanok
The actual adjointable regular operators satisfy
\[
 (\pi_\alpha(b)\xi)(k)=\alpha_{k^{-1}}(b)\xi(k),\qquad
 (\lambda_g\xi)(k)=\xi(g^{-1}k).
\]
They obey the star and group laws, the covariance identity
$\pi_\alpha(\alpha_g(b))\lambda_g=\lambda_g\pi_\alpha(b)$,
and $\|\pi_\alpha(b)\|=\|b\|$, including for nonunital $B$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The coefficient-square order estimate bounds diagonal multiplication in
the actual finite-vector form norm. Extend it and its proved adjoint to
the completion. Translation is the unitary permutation of coordinates.
The coordinate formulas give covariance on a dense set and hence on the
completion. Applying $\pi_\alpha(b)$ to the identity-coordinate vector
with coefficient $b^*$ gives the reverse norm inequality.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}\label{def:reduced-crossed-product}
\uses{def:algebraic-crossed-product,prop:regular-crossed-operators}
\lean{BC4lean.KKTheory.ReducedCrossedProduct,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedProduct_nonUnitalCStarAlgebra,BC4lean.KKTheory.coefficientToReducedCrossed,BC4lean.KKTheory.norm_coefficientToReducedCrossed,BC4lean.KKTheory.algebraicCrossedOperatorHom_eq_sum,BC4lean.KKTheory.algebraicToReducedCrossed,BC4lean.KKTheory.algebraicToReducedCrossed_denseRange}
\leanok
Define $B\rtimes_{\alpha,r}\Gamma$ as the operator-norm closed nonunital
star subalgebra generated by the concrete $\pi_\alpha(b)\lambda_g$ in
$\mathcal L_B(\ell^2(\Gamma,B))$. It is a C$^*$-algebra.
The coefficient inclusion $B\to B\rtimes_{\alpha,r}\Gamma$ is isometric.
Finite twisted convolution integrates by
$f\mapsto\sum_g\pi_\alpha(f(g))\lambda_g$ to a star homomorphism with
dense range in this actual algebra.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:reduced-crossed-norm-separability}
\uses{def:reduced-crossed-product}
\lean{BC4lean.KKTheory.algebraicToReducedCrossed_injective,BC4lean.KKTheory.algebraicCrossedReducedNorm,BC4lean.KKTheory.algebraicCrossedReducedNorm_eq_zero,BC4lean.KKTheory.algebraicCrossedReducedNorm_cstar,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedAlgebra_coe_eq_closure_span,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedProduct_separableSpace}
\leanok
The regular integrated representation is faithful on the finite-support
algebra. Its operator norm is therefore a norm, and satisfies
$\|f^*f\|_r=\|f\|_r^2$. The reduced algebra is the closure of the
actual finite generator span. If $\Gamma$ is countable and $B$ is
separable, the constructed reduced crossed product is separable.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Evaluation of the integrated operator on an identity-coordinate vector
recovers $\alpha_{k^{-1}}(f(k))b$ at coordinate $k$. If the operator
vanishes, take $b=\alpha_{k^{-1}}(f(k))^*$ and use the C$^*$-identity
to show that every coefficient vanishes. The norm and its C$^*$-identity
come from the ambient adjointable operators. Continuous coefficient
families indexed by countably many group elements have separable range;
their complex span and its closure remain separable.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:scalar-reduced-crossed-product}
\uses{def:reduced-crossed-product,def:reduced-group-cstar}
\lean{BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedScalarHilbertEquiv,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedScalarOperatorEquiv_generator,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedScalarProductEquiv,BC4lean.KKTheory.reducedCrossedScalarProductEquiv_oneCoefficient}
\leanok
For the trivial scalar action, the completed regular coefficient module
is unitarily identified with the existing $\ell^2(\Gamma)$. The unitary
sends $z\delta_g$ to the actual scalar delta vector and intertwines
the regular operators. It induces the concrete star-algebra equivalence
\[
 \mathbb C\rtimes_r\Gamma\cong C_r^*(\Gamma),
\]
taking the scalar coefficient identity to the existing reduced
group-algebra unit.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
The finite-vector map $z\delta_g\mapsto z\delta_g$ preserves the
coefficient inner product and therefore the norm. Both finite-vector
images are dense, so it extends to a Hilbert-module unitary.
Conjugation by this unitary sends every crossed-product generator to
$z\lambda_g$. The closed generator spans on the two sides coincide
under this equivalence and its inverse, giving the stated restricted
star-algebra equivalence.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:descent-compact-conjugation}
\uses{def:equivariant-kk}
\lean{BC4lean.KKTheory.EquivariantHilbertModule.adjointableAction,BC4lean.KKTheory.EquivariantHilbertModule.compactOperatorAction,BC4lean.KKTheory.EquivariantHilbertModule.compactOperatorAction_equivariant,BC4lean.KKTheory.EquivariantHilbertModule.compactOperatorAction_rankOne}
\leanok
A compatible equivariant Hilbert $B$-module action $U$ induces actual
conjugation actions on $\mathcal L_B(E)$ and $\mathcal K_B(E)$.
The compact inclusion intertwines them, and
$g\cdot\theta_{x,y}=\theta_{U_gx,U_gy}$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Conjugation by $U_g$, with inverse conjugation by $U_{g^{-1}}$,
is the constructed star-algebra automorphism of adjointable maps.
The proved rank-one transport formula preserves finite-rank spans and
their norm closure. Restrict the automorphism and its inverse to that
compact ideal; the group laws and the equivariance of inclusion follow
from the actual conjugation formulas.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}\label{def:descent-coefficient-module}
\uses{def:reduced-crossed-product,prop:reduced-crossed-norm-separability}
\lean{BC4lean.KKTheory.KasparovDescent.coefficientRepresentation,BC4lean.KKTheory.KasparovDescent.Module,BC4lean.KKTheory.KasparovDescent.countablyGenerated,BC4lean.KKTheory.KasparovDescent.mk_balance,BC4lean.KKTheory.KasparovDescent.mk_inner,BC4lean.KKTheory.KasparovDescent.operatorHom_mk,BC4lean.KKTheory.KasparovDescent.norm_operatorHom_le,BC4lean.KKTheory.KasparovDescent.operatorHom_adjoint,BC4lean.KKTheory.KasparovDescent.operatorHom_compact}
\leanok

construct the completed interior module $E\otimes_B C$ using the actual
coefficient inclusion $B\to C$. Elementary vectors obey the balancing
relation and the inner-product formula
\[
 \langle x\otimes c,y\otimes d\rangle
 =c^*\iota(\langle x,y\rangle)d.
\]
Every adjointable $T$ acts by $T\otimes1$, with its adjoint and norm
bound proved; compact module operators remain compact under this
coefficient extension. If $\Gamma$ is countable, $B$ is separable and
$E$ is countably generated, the constructed module is countably generated.
This prerequisite does not yet construct the integrated source action
or a map on KK groups.
\end{definition}

\section{Cutoff projections}
\begin{proposition}\label{prop:proper-cocompact-cutoff}
\uses{def:cocompact-pieces}
\lean{BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.CutoffFunction,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.exists_cutoffFunction,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.normalized_tsum,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.finite_mixedCoefficient_support,BC4lean.ProperActions.CocompactPiece.proper,BC4lean.ProperActions.CocompactPiece.exists_cutoffFunction}
\leanok
A continuous proper action of a discrete group on a locally compact
Hausdorff space with compact orbit quotient admits a nonnegative
$c\in C_c(X,\mathbb R)$ with
$\sum_g c(g^{-1}x)^2=1$. The sum has finite support at every point,
and its topological sum has the same value. For any two compactly
supported continuous functions $c,d$, the coefficient family
$g\mapsto(x\mapsto c(x)d(g^{-1}x))$ has finite group support.

Every actual closed cocompact piece of a locally compact Hausdorff
proper ambient action inherits properness and admits such a cutoff.
Countability is not needed for these assertions.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Compact orbit representatives admit a nonnegative compactly supported
seed $f$ positive somewhere on every orbit. Properness makes its
translated supports locally finite, so the invariant orbitwise square
sum is continuous, finite and positive. Divide $f$ by its square root.
The resulting finite normalization identity also gives the convergent
topological sum. Compact transporters bound the global support of every
mixed coefficient. On a closed invariant piece, compact transporters
are contained in those of the ambient proper action; apply the same
construction to its compact intrinsic orbit quotient.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:algebraic-cutoff-projection}
\uses{def:algebraic-crossed-product,prop:proper-cocompact-cutoff}
\lean{BC4lean.ProperActions.CutoffCoefficients.convolution_mixed_of_normalized,BC4lean.ProperActions.CutoffCoefficients.involution_mixed,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.mixedAlgebraic,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.coeff_mixedAlgebraic,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.algebraicProjection,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.star_algebraicProjection,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.algebraicProjection_mul_self,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.mixedAlgebraic_partialIsometry}
\leanok
For normalized cutoffs $c,d$, the exact mixed coefficient
$v_{c,d}(g)(x)=c(x)d(g^{-1}x)$ defines a finite-support element of
$C_c(\Gamma,C_0(X))$. The actual twisted convolution and involution
give $v_{c,d}^*=v_{d,c}$ and $v_{c,d}v_{d,e}=v_{c,e}$.
Consequently $p_c=v_{c,c}$ is a self-adjoint idempotent, and the mixed
element implements Murray--von Neumann equivalence of $p_c$ and $p_d$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Global finite support bundles the mixed coefficients in the actual
twisted coefficient algebra. Inversion and conjugation exchange the two
cutoffs. Expanding the coefficient of a product factors out
$c(x)e(g^{-1}x)$ and leaves the normalized sum
$\sum_t d(t^{-1}x)^2=1$. Thus the mixed elements multiply as stated.
Taking equal cutoffs proves the projection identities, and exchanging
the two cutoffs gives both partial-isometry equations.
\end{proof}

\begin{definition}\label{def:nonunital-projection-k0}
\uses{def:operator-k-theory}
\lean{BC4lean.OperatorKTheory.nonunitalProjectionK0,BC4lean.OperatorKTheory.operatorK0Inclusion_nonunitalProjectionK0,BC4lean.OperatorKTheory.nonunitalProjectionK0_eq_of_partialIsometry}
\leanok
An actual projection in a possibly nonunital C$^*$-algebra $A$ defines
its singleton-matrix projection class in the forced unitization.
The scalar quotient of that matrix is zero, so the class belongs to
the existing scalar-kernel group $K_0(A)$. An actual partial isometry
between two projections proves equality of these classes.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:cutoff-projection}
\uses{prop:proper-cocompact-cutoff,prop:algebraic-cutoff-projection,def:reduced-crossed-product,def:nonunital-projection-k0}
\lean{BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.exists_cutoffFunction,BC4lean.ProperActions.CocompactPiece.exists_cutoffFunction,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.reducedCutoffProjection,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.reducedCutoffProjection_isStarProjection,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.reducedCutoffProjection_partialIsometry,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.cutoffK0,BC4lean.ProperActions.Cutoff.cutoffK0_independent}
\leanok
For a locally compact Hausdorff proper cocompact $\Gamma$-space,
choose $c\in C_c(X,[0,\infty))$ with $\sum_g c(g^{-1}x)^2=1$ and set
$p_c(g)(x)=c(x)c(g^{-1}x)$. Such a cutoff exists, $p_c$ is a projection
in $C_0(X)\rtimes_r\Gamma$, and its K-class is independent of $c$.
The class is in the actual nonunital operator $K_0$ group of the
constructed reduced crossed product. Its independence follows from
the explicit mixed-cutoff partial isometry in that completed algebra.
\end{definition}
