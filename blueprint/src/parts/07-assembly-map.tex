\chapter{Construction of the assembly map}
\label{ch:assembly-map}

Let $\Gamma$ be a countable discrete group for the KK-theoretic targets.
The reduced crossed-product and cutoff prerequisites hold for arbitrary
discrete groups unless countability is stated. Coefficient C$^*$-algebras
may be nonunital; retain the separability hypotheses used by the existing KK
quotients and products. Assembly spaces have continuous proper actions with
the stated locally compact Hausdorff and second-countability hypotheses.

The constructions below give reduced descent and the coefficient-free
assembly homomorphisms in both degrees under these hypotheses. Construction
of these homomorphisms does not assert that they are isomorphisms.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:proper-cocompact-cutoff}

and its topological sum has the same value. For any two compactly
supported continuous functions $c,d$, the coefficient family
$g\mapsto(x\mapsto c(x)d(g^{-1}x))$ has finite group support.

Every actual closed cocompact piece of a locally compact Hausdorff
proper ambient action inherits properness and admits such a cutoff.
Countability is not needed for these assertions.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Compact orbit representatives admit a nonnegative compactly supported
seed $f$ positive somewhere on every orbit. Properness makes its
translated supports locally finite, so the invariant orbitwise square
sum is continuous, finite and positive. Divide $f$ by its square root.
The resulting finite normalization identity also gives the convergent
topological sum. Compact transporters bound the global support of every
mixed coefficient. On a closed invariant piece, compact transporters
are contained in those of the ambient proper action; apply the same
construction to its compact intrinsic orbit quotient.
\end{proof}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:assembly-cofinal-index}
\uses{def:assembly-map,prop:piece-assembly-transition,def:topological-side}
\lean{BC4lean.Assembly.indexedAssemblyHom,BC4lean.Assembly.indexedAssemblyHom_of,BC4lean.Assembly.assemblyHom_reindex,BC4lean.Assembly.assemblyHom_cofinalIndexEquiv,BC4lean.Assembly.indexedAssemblyHom_cofinalComparisonEquiv}
\leanok
Let $\varphi:J\to\mathcal C_\Gamma(X)$ be a monotone map from a nonempty
directed preorder to the actual cocompact pieces. The restricted assembly is
the colimit of the same actual piece family. If
\[
 \forall S\in\mathcal C_\Gamma(X)\ \exists j\in J,\quad S\leq\varphi(j),
\]
the established cofinal indexing equivalence preserves its assembly value.
The comparison between two such genuinely cofinal systems does likewise.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
\leanok
Evaluate the restricted colimit map on a piece representative. The actual
Chapter 6 reindex map sends it to the same piece of the full diagram, so the
piece formula proves the assembly equality. Identifying the colimits retains
the essential upper-bound cofinality hypothesis. For two cofinal systems,
compose the first equivalence with the inverse of the second and cancel by
the actual additive-equivalence laws. Mere pointwise coverage of the ambient
space cannot replace cofinality.
\end{proof}
